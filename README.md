# Social Media Campaign Pack: Diwali Festive Sale

A complete **social media campaign design** for a fictional home-decor brand, **Lumi Home**. It includes 10 feed posts, 3 stories and 1 poster that share one visual style, so the whole campaign looks connected.

![Post 01](posts/post-01.png)

## What is included
| Format | Count | Size | Folder |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram / Facebook posts | 10 | 1080 × 1080 px | `posts/` |
| Instagram / WhatsApp stories | 3 | 1080 × 1920 px | `stories/` |
| Printable poster | 1 | 1240 × 1754 px (A-series ratio) | `poster/` |

Every design is provided as an editable **SVG** and an exported **PNG**.

## Campaign plan (suggested posting order)
| Post | Message | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Light Up Your Home | Announce the sale |
| 2 | 3 Days To Go | Build excitement |
| 3 to 5 | Diyas, Fairy Lights, Rangoli Kits | Show best-selling products |
| 6 | Buy 2 Get 1 Free | Share an offer |
| 7 | Glow Up Your Doorway | Give a useful decor tip |
| 8 | Happy Diwali | Send warm wishes |
| 9 | Free Delivery | Remove a buying barrier |
| 10 | Sale Ends Tonight | Create urgency |

Stories: **Sale is live**, **Top offers**, **Last chance**.

## Design decisions
- **Colours:** deep purple (night sky) with marigold and saffron (festival lights), plus magenta for offers
- **Hero image:** a glowing diya (oil lamp) that appears in every design, so people recognise the campaign instantly
- **Typography:** large serif headlines for impact, clean sans-serif for details
- **Hierarchy:** headline first, offer badge second, "Shop Now" button last
- **Rhythm:** alternating dark and bright backgrounds keep the feed interesting while the layout stays consistent
